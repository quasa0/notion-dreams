# Mike Vernal

Role from hackathon doc: Investor, Conviction.

## Public Signal

- TechCrunch reported Mike Vernal joined Conviction as a GP when Conviction raised a $230M second fund.
- His background includes nearly seven years at Sequoia and eight years at Facebook as VP of product and engineering.
- TechCrunch notes he led or was involved with high-profile investments including Notion, Rippling, and Verkada.
- Public profiles describe Conviction as AI-focused and oriented around AI-native "Software 3.0" companies.

## What They Do

AI/startup investor with deep product/engineering operating experience. He can evaluate both company potential and technical/product execution.

## Likely Idea Preferences

High confidence:

- AI-native software with strong founder-market insight
- workflows where AI changes the product architecture, not just adds chat
- clear market wedge and buyer pain
- products that feel inevitable if they work
- technical teams that can move fast and explain tradeoffs

## Recent Themes

Conviction's public thesis is that powerful AI models are still early in being translated into products that transform industries. Startups can move faster than incumbents.

## Demo Angles That May Land

- "This is the wedge into replacing a whole category of workflow software."
- Pick one persona and show deep workflow ownership.
- Show why a startup could distribute this through Notion rather than fighting every incumbent head-on.

## Risk

Investors see many AI demos. A generic "agent for productivity" needs a sharper wedge.
