## What They Do

Co-founded and helped build Notion. Public signal suggests deep technical/product taste and attachment to Notion's original composable-tool vision.

## Likely Idea Preferences

High confidence:

- composable tools that let users build their own workflows
- simple primitives with surprising expressive power
- technical elegance and product restraint
- projects that make Notion feel like programmable software for everyone
- lean, real implementation over overproduced pitch

## Recent Themes

Notion's historical and current throughline: blocks, databases, docs, AI, and now Workers/agents as extensible primitives for people to build their own systems.

## Demo Angles That May Land

- Build a primitive or template that many users can adapt, not a single hardcoded workflow.
- Show how Workers make Notion more like a programmable workspace.
- Use a small set of primitives elegantly: database, page, tool, trigger, memory.

## Risk

A complicated app that ignores Notion's composability may miss the core product philosophy.
